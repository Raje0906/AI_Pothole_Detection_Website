About Us

The Pothole Detection System is an innovative solution designed to improve road safety and maintenance efficiency. Our system uses advanced AI and machine learning algorithms to detect and analyze potholes in roads through images and videos.

Our mission is to:

  • Enhance road safety for all users
  • Reduce vehicle damage caused by potholes
  • Help authorities prioritize road repairs
  • Create a community-driven approach to road maintenance

How It Works

Upload

Submit images or videos of road conditions through our user-friendly interface.

Analyze

Our AI model processes the media to detect and analyze potholes with high accuracy.

Report

Receive detailed reports with pothole locations and severity assessments.

Track

Monitor detection history and view locations on interactive maps.
